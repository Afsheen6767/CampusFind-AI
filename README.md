# 🎓 CampusFind AI

> An AI-powered Lost & Found platform designed specifically for university campuses to reconnect students with their lost personal belongings.

---

## 🔗 Live Deployed App
👉 **Live App Link:** [https://campusfind-ai.ai.studio](https://campusfind-ai.ai.studio)
https://campus-find-ai-ptp5.vercel.app/
---

## 📌 Problem & Target Audience
* **The Real Problem:** University campuses are large and busy environments. Students frequently lose critical personal items such as ID cards, keys, wallets, smartphones, and notebooks. Traditional lost-and-found methods (physical notice boards or crowded WhatsApp groups) are slow, unorganized, and inefficient.
* **Target Audience:** University students, faculty, and campus security staff.
* **The Solution:** CampusFind AI centralizes lost and found reports into a single, smart digital hub. It uses artificial intelligence to compare reports and automatically match lost items with found items on campus.

---

## ✨ Features List
- 🔐 **User Authentication:** Student & Staff account registration and secure login.
- 📝 **Report Lost Item:** Form to log missing belongings with item details, category, date, and campus location.
- 🔎 **Report Found Item:** Easy logging system for items found around campus.
- 📋 **Browse Listings:** Live view of all reported lost and found items.
- 🤖 **AI Matcher Engine:** Automated AI functionality to compare and suggest potential item matches.
- 👤 **User Dashboard:** Profile view to manage submitted reports.

---

## 🤖 AI Feature & System Instructions

* **What the AI Feature Does:** The built-in AI analyzes descriptions, categories, colors, and location data of reported lost items, comparing them against the database of found items to calculate match probability and highlight potential matches.
* **System Prompt / Instructions Used:**
> *"You are an AI assistant for CampusFind AI. Your job is to analyze lost and found item reports. Compare the description, item category, color, location found/lost, and time. Determine if there is a match between a lost item report and a found item record. Provide a match confidence rating (High/Medium/Low) and explain why they match."*
